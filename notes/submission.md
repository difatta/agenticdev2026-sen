# Submission notes — ACM SIGSOFT Software Engineering Notes (SEN)

- **Invitation:** SEN editorial team invited the organisers to submit a workshop summary.
- **Deadline:** **30 November 2026** for the January 2027 issue (later submissions go to a subsequent issue).
- **Send to:** editors_SIGSOFT@acm.org, in PDF, Word or LaTeX. State the workshop acronym and full name:
  *AgenticDev — 1st International Workshop on Agentic AI for Next-Generation Software Development*.
- **Guidelines:** https://www2.sigsoft.org/sen/guideline/ · organiser instructions: https://www2.sigsoft.org/sen/instructions/
- **Template:** SEN LaTeX class `acm_sen_article.cls` + `latex-example.zip` (both from the guideline page). The class is in the repo root; bibliography style `acm`.
- **Required content (summaries):** title and authors (affiliations, emails); abstract (printed in SEN hardcopy); full summary (ACM DL).
- **Other requirements:** source files + PDF with all fonts embedded; keywords ideally from ACM CCS; SEN is edited, not refereed; authors keep copyright, grant ACM non-exclusive rights; any copied text beyond a few words must be quoted and cited.
- **Length:** no explicit limit for summaries (columns are normally ≤ 4 pages). Agreed target: 4 pages.
- **Examples:** three SEN July 2026 summaries (BRIDGES, MSSiS, RSE): 3–4 pages; organisation + stats, per-paper or grouped summaries, panel, lessons/agenda, next steps, refs to all papers.

## Workshop facts (from the proceedings front matter)
- 12 October 2026, Munich, co-located with ASE 2026. Proceedings: ACM, ISBN 979-8-4007-2985-0.
- Sponsors: ACM SIGSOFT, ACM SIGAI, IEEE CS.
- Inspired by Horizon Europe projects AI4SWEng, ASTIR, MOSAICO (HIVEMIND is mentioned only as a member of the AGenSE Alliance, in the panel section; not acknowledged).
- 84 submissions (47 long, 36 short, 1 demo); ≥ 2 reviews each.
- 26 accepted (11 long, 8 short, 7 extended abstracts), acceptance rate 31%. The foreword's "25 / 6 extended abstracts" is a slip, confirmed against the programme.
- Programme: panel 09:00–10:00; 15 papers in five sessions (code analysis, agentic coding, testing, repair, agentic orchestration); 7 posters; 4 accepted papers not presented in person.
- Panel (moderator Giuseppe Di Fatta): Ali Atalay (AI4SWEng), Anh Tuan Le (ASTIR), Ioannis Kyprakis (FAITH), Jean-Marie Mottu and Tuong Manh Vu (MOSAICO).
