\section{Concluding Remarks}
\label{sec:conclusion}

AgenticDev~2026 showed strong interest in agentic software engineering from both research and industry, and a community ready to examine agents with the same rigour applied to any other engineering artefact. Across the papers and the panel, progress depended less on more capable models than on the engineering around them: harnesses and contracts, provenance and authority, and evaluation that reflects real cost and the full lifecycle. The accepted papers are available in the ACM workshop proceedings.
