\section{Organisation and Programme}
\label{sec:workshop}

The organisation co-chairs of AgenticDev~2026 were Giuseppe Di Fatta (Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, Italy), Atta Badii (University of Reading, UK) and Ali Atalay (AI4SEC, Estonia). Andrea Rosani (Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, Italy), Jean-Marie Mottu (Universit\'e de Nantes -- LS2N -- IMT Atlantique, France) and Simos Gerasimou (Cyprus University of Technology, Cyprus) served as programme co-chairs. The technical programme committee had 31 members.

%The call invited long papers, short papers and demonstrations. 
The workshop received 84 submissions. 
%(47 long, 36 short and one demonstration). 
Each submission was reviewed by at least two of its TPC members for relevance, originality, technical quality, clarity and potential impact. Twenty-six contributions were accepted: 11 long papers, 8 short papers and 7 posters/extended abstracts, all published in the ACM workshop proceedings. The overall acceptance rate (inlcuding posters) was 31\%, while for only long and short papers it was 22.6\%.
Their authors came from academia and industry, with affiliations in 21 countries across Europe, Asia and the Americas.

The full-day programme opened with the panel described in Section~\ref{sec:panel}. The papers were presented in five sessions, on code analysis, agentic coding, testing, repair and agentic orchestration. The seven extended abstracts were presented as posters. The workshop attracted about 100 participants.
