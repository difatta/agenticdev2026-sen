\section{Organisation and Programme}
\label{sec:workshop}

AgenticDev~2026 was organised by Giuseppe Di Fatta (Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, Italy), Atta Badii (University of Reading, UK) and Ali Atalay (AI4SEC, Estonia). Andrea Rosani (Free University of Bozen-Bolzano, Italy), Jean-Marie Mottu (Universit\'e de Nantes -- LS2N -- IMT Atlantique, France) and Simos Gerasimou (Cyprus University of Technology, Cyprus) served as programme co-chairs. The technical programme committee had 31 members.

The call invited long papers, short papers and demonstrations. The workshop received 84 submissions (47 long, 36 short and one demonstration). Each submission was reviewed by at least two of its members for relevance, originality, technical quality, clarity and potential impact. Twenty-six papers were accepted, an acceptance rate of 31\%: 11 long papers, 8 short papers and 7 extended abstracts, all published in the ACM workshop proceedings. Their authors came from academia and industry, with affiliations in 21 countries across Europe, Asia and the Americas.

The full-day programme opened with the panel described in Section~\ref{sec:panel}. Fifteen papers were presented in five sessions, on code analysis, agentic coding, testing, repair and agentic orchestration. The seven extended abstracts were presented as posters, and four further accepted papers appear in the proceedings without an in-person presentation. The workshop attracted about 100 participants.
